Frequently asked questions

Before taking the first step

Can I buy or sell from abroad?

Yes. With a duly registered power of attorney, or an apostilled one if granted abroad, I can represent you through the entire process. I keep you up to date with written reports and video calls at every stage.

How is my property's price determined?

Through a comparative analysis of the market in your area, the condition of the property and its documentation. You receive a supported valuation, not a number pulled from thin air.

What documents do I need to sell?

Title deed, cadastral certificate, tax clearances and registry records, among others depending on the case. In our first consultation we review your file together and build the exact list.

Do you only work in Maracaibo?

Maracaibo is my base and my specialty. For transactions in other states I advise you all the same: I count on the reach of the RE/MAX network across Venezuela and coordinate with local agents when needed.

Do you also assist in Spanish?

Yes. I work in English and Spanish, with foreign investors as well as Venezuelans abroad who prefer to handle the transaction in either language.
