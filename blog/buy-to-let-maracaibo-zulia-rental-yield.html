Buy-to-let in Maracaibo and Zulia: how to calculate rental yield

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 5 min read

To calculate the yield of buying to let in Maracaibo or Zulia, divide the annual rental income by the purchase price and multiply by 100. That percentage, your gross yield, is the first number you should know before investing.

Buying to let is one of the most direct ways to put your capital to work in Zulia. But not every property yields the same, and the purchase price doesn't tell the whole story. These are the numbers that really matter.

The formula, in one line

Gross yield = (monthly rent × 12) ÷ purchase price × 100.

Example: a $60,000 apartment renting at $500 a month earns $6,000 a year. 6,000 ÷ 60,000 × 100 = 10% gross yield.

From gross to net yield

Net yield is what actually reaches your pocket. It subtracts:

  • Condo fees and maintenance.
  • Empty months (vacancy).
  • Repairs and turnaround between tenants.
  • Your management time (or the cost of managing it for you).

A prudent calculation subtracts one to two months of rent per year to estimate the net realistically. In the example, subtracting one month ($500), effective rent drops to $5,500 and the net is around 9%.

How to choose a rental property, step by step

  1. Set your budget and yield target.
  2. Filter by areas with steady rental demand in Zulia.
  3. Compare expected rent against the purchase price.
  4. Verify the documentation before negotiating.
  5. Project the net by subtracting vacancy and costs.

Mistakes that lower your return

  • Overpaying out of haste.
  • Ignoring vacancy months.
  • Buying in an area with no demand.
  • Neglecting documentation and ending up with fragile contracts.

A rental investment is decided with numbers, not hunches.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good yield in Maracaibo?

It depends on the area and condition, but always compare annual rent against the purchase price before deciding.

What currency is the yield calculated in?

In Maracaibo and Zulia, transactions and rents are usually set in US dollars, so yield is calculated in that currency.

Is residential or commercial better for renting?

Both work; residential tends to have steadier demand and commercial higher rents. It depends on your profile and capital.

How do I estimate vacancy?

A prudent approach is to subtract one or two months of rent per year, depending on the area and demand.

Can I buy to let from abroad?

Yes, with an apostilled power of attorney and support to verify, buy and then manage the rental.
