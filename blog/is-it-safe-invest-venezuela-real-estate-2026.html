Is it safe to invest in Venezuelan real estate in 2026?

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 5 min read

Yes, investing in Venezuelan real estate can be safe and profitable in 2026, as long as you verify the property's paperwork and work with an advisor who answers for every step. An investment's safety depends less on the country than on the process by which you buy.

It is the question every investor asks, inside and outside the country. And it deserves an honest answer, not a sales pitch. The truth is that most problems in a purchase do not come from the market but from buying without verifying. With the right process, risk is measured and controlled.

Safety depends on the process, not the country

A property with clean documentation (title deed, cadastral certificate, tax clearances and registry record) is a solid investment. One without proper paperwork is a risk, anywhere in the world. Your investment's protection lies in due diligence, not luck.

How to invest safely, step by step

  1. Verify ownership and that the property is free of liens.
  2. Review the full file before negotiating price.
  3. Structure the payment with documentary guarantees at each stage.
  4. Calculate the real yield before buying, not after.
  5. Formalize before the registry with professional support.

Why Maracaibo and Zulia interest investors

  • Attractive dollar prices: price per square meter does not yet reflect its full potential.
  • Steady rental demand: commerce, universities and the diaspora keep the market active.
  • Modern supply: renovated apartments and commercial spaces ready to rent.

Can a foreigner invest?

Yes. A foreigner can buy property in Venezuela with valid ID and, in some cases, a tax ID (RIF). If not in the country, the transaction is handled with an apostilled power of attorney. The verification and protection process is the same as for a national.

Investing in Venezuela is not gambling: it is analysis. With the right process, safety is not a promise, it is a procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner legally buy property in Venezuela?

Yes. A foreigner can buy with valid ID and, in some cases, a tax ID (RIF). The process can be handled with a power of attorney if you are not in the country.

What currency is used to buy?

Most transactions in Maracaibo and Zulia are agreed and valued in US dollars.

What is the biggest risk when investing?

Buying without verifying the documentation. With proper due diligence, that risk is almost entirely eliminated.

How do I protect my money during the purchase?

By structuring the payment with documentary guarantees at each stage and not releasing funds without backing.

Is it better to invest now or wait?

The current appeal is dollar prices below their potential; the decision depends on your goal and finding a verified property.
