Is it safe to invest in Venezuelan real estate in 2026?

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia

Yes, investing in Venezuelan real estate can be safe and profitable in 2026, as long as you verify the property's paperwork and work with an advisor who answers for every step. The Maracaibo and Zulia market offers dollar prices below their potential and steady rental demand.

Safety is not about the country, it's about the process

Most problems in a purchase do not come from the market but from buying without verifying. A property with clean documentation (title deed, cadastral certificate, tax clearances and registry records) is a solid investment. One without proper paperwork is a risk, anywhere in the world.

Why Maracaibo and Zulia interest investors

  • Attractive dollar prices: price per square meter does not yet reflect the full potential.
  • Steady rental demand: commerce, universities and the diaspora keep the market active.
  • Modern supply: renovated apartments and commercial spaces ready to rent.

How to invest safely, step by step

  • Verify ownership and that the property is free of liens.
  • Structure the payment with documentary guarantees at every stage.
  • Calculate the real yield before buying, not after.
  • Work with a backed advisor, in my case the RE/MAX network.

Investing in Venezuela is not gambling: it is analysis. With the right process, risk is measured and controlled.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner legally buy property in Venezuela?

Yes. A foreigner can buy property in Venezuela; valid ID and, in some cases, a tax ID (RIF) are required. The process can be handled with a power of attorney if you are not in the country.

What currency is used to buy?

Most real estate transactions in Maracaibo and Zulia are agreed and valued in US dollars.

What is the biggest risk when investing?

Buying without verifying the documentation. With proper due diligence, that risk is almost entirely eliminated.
