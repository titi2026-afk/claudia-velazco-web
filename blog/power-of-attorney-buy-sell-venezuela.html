Power of attorney to buy or sell in Venezuela: registered vs. apostilled

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 5 min read

You need a registered power of attorney when you grant it inside Venezuela and an apostilled one when you sign it abroad. Both let a trusted representative buy or sell your property in Maracaibo, or anywhere in Zulia, without you traveling.

The power of attorney is the document that makes operating at a distance possible. Without it, no purchase or sale can close if you are not present. Understanding the difference between a registered and an apostilled power saves you time and prevents a badly done step from stalling your transaction.

What is a power of attorney and what is it for?

A power of attorney legally authorizes someone to act in your name: to sign the purchase or sale, obtain tax clearances, collect payment or hand over keys. You define what they can and cannot do.

Registered power: when you grant it in Venezuela

If you are visiting the country, you grant the power before a Venezuelan notary or registry. It is valid immediately to operate in Maracaibo and across Zulia. It is the most direct route if it coincides with a trip.

Apostilled power: when you sign it abroad

If you live in the United States, Spain, Chile, Colombia or any Hague Convention country, this is your route:

  1. Draft the power with the exact faculties (buy, sell, collect, sign).
  2. Sign it before a notary in your country of residence.
  3. Apostille it before the competent authority (the apostille certifies international validity).
  4. Send it to Venezuela; your representative records it and can then act.

If your country is not in the Hague Convention, the document is legalized through the consulate instead of apostilled. The legal effect is the same.

Tip: the power should be tailored to your transaction. Too broad is an unnecessary risk; too narrow can stall the closing. Before you sign anything, check that it says exactly what you need.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my house in Venezuela without traveling?

Yes. With an apostilled power of attorney signed in your country of residence, a trusted representative sells your property in Maracaibo or across Zulia while you sign remotely.

How long does it take to apostille a power of attorney?

It varies by country, but usually a few days to two weeks. Start it as soon as you decide to buy or sell.

Is an apostilled power valid across all of Venezuela?

Yes. Once recorded it is valid nationwide, not only in Zulia.

What if my country is not in the Hague Convention?

It is legalized through the consulate instead of apostilled; the legal effect is the same.

Can I revoke the power afterward?

Yes, a power of attorney can be revoked following the corresponding legal procedure.
