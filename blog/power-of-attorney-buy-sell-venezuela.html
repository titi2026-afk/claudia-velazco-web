Power of attorney to buy or sell in Venezuela: registered vs. apostilled

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia

If you are outside Venezuela, you need a registered power of attorney when you grant it inside the country and an apostilled power of attorney when you sign it abroad. Both let a trusted representative buy or sell your property in Maracaibo, or anywhere in Zulia, without you traveling.

What is a power of attorney and why you need one

A power of attorney is the legal document that authorizes someone to act in your name: to sign the purchase or sale, obtain tax clearances or hand over the keys. Without it, no transaction can close remotely.

Registered power: when you grant it in Venezuela

If you are visiting the country, you grant the power before a Venezuelan notary or registry. It is valid immediately to operate in Maracaibo and across Zulia.

Apostilled power: when you sign it abroad

If you live in the United States, Spain, Chile or any country in the Hague Convention, you sign the power before a local notary and then have it apostilled. The apostille certifies the document is internationally valid. If your country is not in the Convention, it is done through the consulate (legalization).

  • Draft the power with the exact faculties (buy, sell, collect, sign).
  • Sign it before a notary in your country of residence.
  • Apostille it (or legalize it at the Venezuelan consulate).
  • Send it to Venezuela; your representative records it and can then act.

One tip before you sign

The power should be tailored to your transaction. Too broad is a risk; too narrow can stall the closing. Before you sign anything, we talk it through and make sure it says exactly what you need.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my house in Venezuela without traveling?

Yes. With an apostilled power of attorney signed in your country of residence, a trusted representative can sell your property in Maracaibo or across Zulia while you sign remotely.

How long does it take to apostille a power of attorney?

It varies by country, but usually a few days to two weeks. Start it as soon as you decide to buy or sell.

Is an apostilled power valid across all of Venezuela?

Yes. Once recorded in Venezuela it is valid nationwide, not only in Zulia.
