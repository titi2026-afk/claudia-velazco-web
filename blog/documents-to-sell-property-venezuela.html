Legal guide

What documents you need to sell a property in Venezuela

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 5 min read

To sell a property in Venezuela you need, at a minimum, the title deed, the cadastral certificate, up-to-date tax clearances and the registry record. In Maracaibo and Zulia, having this file ready from the start speeds up the sale and avoids surprises.

A serious buyer wants to move fast. If your documents are ready, the sale flows; if any are missing, it cools off. Having the file in order is often the difference between selling and waiting. Here's the list and how to prepare it.

The seller's basic file

  • Title deed duly registered in your name.
  • Cadastral certificate in force, issued by the municipality.
  • Tax clearances for utilities and municipal taxes, up to date.
  • Registry record or lien certification.
  • Owner's ID and tax ID (RIF) (or a power of attorney, if selling remotely).

Documents you may be asked for, depending on the case

SituationAdditional document
Property in a condominiumCondominium clearance
Came from an inheritanceEstate declaration
Selling from abroadApostilled power of attorney
There was a mortgageMortgage release

How to prepare your file, step by step

  1. Gather the title deed and verify it's in your name and registered.
  2. Obtain or update the cadastral certificate at the municipality.
  3. Get current on utility and municipal tax clearances.
  4. Request the registry record or lien certification.
  5. Check whether your case needs additional documents (inheritance, condo, mortgage).

Tip: prepare the file before listing the property. Going to market with everything in order gives you negotiating power and prevents an interested buyer from cooling off over delays.

Selling from abroad

If you're outside the country, you don't need to travel to sell. With an apostilled power of attorney, a representative signs the sale for you in Maracaibo or across Zulia, while you follow the process by video call and written reports.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell without the cadastral certificate?

It is not advisable. The cadastral certificate is a key document to record the sale; without it the process stalls.

What if the property came from an inheritance?

You'll need the estate declaration in addition to the basic file. It's a common step and is handled in order.

Can I sell from abroad?

Yes, with an apostilled power of attorney a representative signs the sale for you in Maracaibo or across Zulia.

How long does it take to gather the documents?

It depends on each procedure, but starting early prevents the sale from stalling when the buyer appears.

Do I need a tax ID (RIF) to sell?

Yes, the owner's RIF is usually required within the sale documentation.
