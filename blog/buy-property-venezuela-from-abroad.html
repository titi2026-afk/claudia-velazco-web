For the diaspora

How to buy property in Venezuela from abroad

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 6 min read

To buy property in Venezuela from abroad you need four things: a power of attorney so a representative can sign for you, legal verification of the property, a payment structure with guarantees, and an advisor who answers for every step. With that, a Venezuelan or foreign investor in the US, Spain, Chile or Colombia can buy in Maracaibo or across Zulia without boarding a plane.

Every year more Venezuelans abroad, along with foreign investors, buy in the country without traveling. Distance is no longer an obstacle: the transaction runs on video calls, digital documents and a legal power of attorney. What makes the difference is not being present, but having the right process and a trusted person on the ground. This guide walks you through that process step by step.

The process, step by step

  1. Define what you want and your budget in dollars. Area, property type (apartment, house, commercial unit), square meters, bedrooms and purpose: to live in, rent out or protect capital.
  2. Receive a filtered shortlist. Instead of scrolling hundreds of listings, your advisor sends only the properties that match your criteria, with photos, video and a fact sheet.
  3. Take the virtual tour. A live video call for each property you like, to see it as if you were there and ask anything.
  4. Verify the documentation before negotiating. Title deed, cadastral certificate, tax clearances and registry record. This step prevents almost every problem.
  5. Grant the power of attorney. Registered if signed in Venezuela, apostilled if signed in your country of residence (see below).
  6. Structure the payment with guarantees. Money never changes hands without documentary backing; every stage is recorded in writing.
  7. Sign and record. Your representative signs the purchase before the registry and receives the keys in your name.

The power of attorney: your signature at a distance

The power of attorney is the legal instrument that lets a trusted third party sign the purchase for you. There are two paths depending on where you sign it:

  • Registered power: if you are visiting Venezuela, you grant it before a notary or registry and it is valid immediately.
  • Apostilled power: if you live abroad, you sign it before a local notary and then apostille it (Hague Convention). If your country is not in the convention, it is legalized through the consulate.

The power should be tailored: with the exact faculties to buy, pay and sign, no more and no less. Too broad is an unnecessary risk.

Verification: what is checked before you pay

Buying without verifying is the mistake that costs dearly. Before a single dollar moves, the property is confirmed free of liens and its file complete:

  • Duly registered title deed.
  • Cadastral certificate in force.
  • Utility and municipal tax clearances.
  • Registry record or lien certification.

A real example of how the transaction looks

A Venezuelan family in Madrid wanted an apartment in Bella Vista, Maracaibo, to rent out. They received six filtered options, took three virtual tours, chose one, we verified the documentation, they signed an apostilled power in Spain, and the purchase closed in Maracaibo with written reports at every step. They never traveled.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Paying before verifying ownership and liens.
  • Signing a generic power downloaded online instead of one drafted for your transaction.
  • Trusting photos without a live tour.
  • Working without an advisor who reports in writing at every stage.

Buying from abroad does not mean buying blind: it means having someone you trust who sees what you cannot see in person, and who answers for every step.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy property in Venezuela without traveling?

Yes. With an apostilled power of attorney signed in your country of residence, a trusted representative buys and signs for you in Maracaibo or across Zulia, while you follow every step by video call and written reports.

How long does it take to buy from abroad?

It depends on the property and the apostille of the power, but a transaction with clean paperwork usually closes within a few weeks.

What currency is used to buy?

Most real estate transactions in Maracaibo and Zulia are agreed and paid in US dollars.

Is it safe to send money to buy?

Yes, if the transaction is structured with documentary guarantees at every stage and money is never released without backing. That protection is part of the service.

What if I can't sign the power in person?

You sign it before a notary in your city of residence and apostille it; that document is legally valid in Venezuela.
