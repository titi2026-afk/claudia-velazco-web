Coming home

Moving back to Venezuela: your home, sorted

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo

Moving back to Venezuela is a decision of the heart, but also of logistics. And of all the pieces to sort out, your home is the one that brings the most peace of mind to have ready before you arrive. Here are the keys to returning to a home, not to uncertainty.

Sort out your home before you travel

You can buy or rent your home remotely, using the same tools investors use: video calls, virtual tours and a trusted representative who verifies every detail. So when you land, your keys are waiting.

Buy or rent when you return?

  • Renting first gives you time to get to know the city and decide calmly where to put down roots.
  • Buying makes sense if you already have clarity about the area and want to secure your wealth in dollars.

Areas to start over

Bella Vista, Virginia and La Lago offer services, relative security and good connectivity, ideal for those returning who want stability from day one.
