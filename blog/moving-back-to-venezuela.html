Coming home

Moving back to Venezuela: a guide to sorting out your home

Claudia Velazco · RE/MAX Eleva, Maracaibo, Zulia · 5 min read

Moving back to Venezuela is easier when you sort out your home before you arrive. You can buy or rent your home remotely in Maracaibo or Zulia, with virtual tours and a trusted representative, so your keys are waiting when you land.

Moving back to Venezuela is a decision of the heart, but also of logistics. And of all the pieces to sort out, your home is the one that brings the most peace of mind to have ready before you arrive. Here are the keys to returning to a home, not to uncertainty.

Sort out your home before you travel

You don't have to wait until you're in the country to find where to live. With the same tools diaspora investors use, you can secure your home remotely:

  1. Define your area, budget and whether you'll buy or rent.
  2. Receive filtered options with photos, video and a fact sheet.
  3. Take live virtual tours of the ones you like.
  4. Review the contract or purchase documentation with your advisor.
  5. Sign remotely (lease or power of attorney to buy) and get the keys on arrival.

For most people returning after several years, renting first for a few months is the safest route: you reconnect with the city and decide your purchase on familiar ground.

Zulia areas to start over

  • Bella Vista: central, with services and shops on foot.
  • Virginia: residential, quiet, ideal for families.
  • La Lago: exclusive, lakefront, for those who want the best.
  • El Milagro: open views and the lake breeze.

Beyond the keys

Returning also means reactivating services, understanding daily life and reintegrating into the city. Having your home sorted frees you to focus on everything else. And if you'll keep a property you already own in Venezuela, it can also be managed for you while you finish your move.

Coming home is a reunion. Let your house be the easy part of the return.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find a home in Maracaibo before I move?

Yes. It's coordinated by video call, with virtual tours and a reviewed lease or purchase, so you arrive to a home that's ready.

Should I rent or buy when I return?

If you've been away for years, renting first gives you time to resettle. Buying makes sense if you already know the area and want to secure your wealth.

How long should I rent before buying?

There's no fixed rule, but a few months usually suffice to get to know the city and decide calmly.

Can I buy my home remotely before arriving?

Yes, with an apostilled power of attorney a representative signs the purchase for you in Maracaibo or Zulia.

Which Zulia areas are best to return to?

Bella Vista, Virginia and La Lago offer services, connectivity and stability, ideal for those returning.
